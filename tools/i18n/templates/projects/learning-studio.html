A clear visual language

Editorial typography, warm colours, original vector illustrations and subtle motion give the platform a calm, approachable identity across desktop and mobile.

Learning through choices

A six-step workshop observation sequence includes two choice-based activities, immediate feedback for every answer, locally saved progress and a downloadable summary.
