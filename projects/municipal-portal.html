A useful website, with a sense of place.

Built around everyday needs

A resident should not have to understand a municipal organisation chart to find a service. Civil records, school information, room bookings and appointments are given direct, clearly labelled entry points.

The project combines a complete editorial website with working service simulations. The fictional town has its own neighbourhoods, river, public places and visual identity.

Editorial, rather than ornamental

The original river mark, deep civic blue, olive green and restrained terracotta establish a recognisable system. Large serif headings and varied layouts give news, practical information and territorial stories their own rhythm.

The design research reviewed Sens, Les Deux Alpes, Zonza, Courtenay and Parramatta. Their approaches informed the research; no layout, logo or editorial content was copied.

Citizen services that actually respond.

Find information

Accent-insensitive, relevance-ranked global search with instant suggestions and content-type filters. A searchable directory and a custom interactive map provide complementary access to places.

Plan and participate

List and calendar agenda views, category filters, month navigation and ICS exports. Room and appointment journeys validate availability before saving a local request.

Follow a request

A reporting flow includes a fictional map location and an example attachment. The citizen dashboard reflects status changes and notifications made in the administration demo.

Content management people can understand.

From a draft to a live page

Create an article, choose a photograph, preview it, save a draft and publish. Changes appear on the public site in the same browser, including a second open tab.

The administration also manages events, pages, municipal alerts, directory entries, rooms, manual availability blocks and simulated citizen requests.

Honest demonstration boundaries

This is a local CMS simulation. There is no server authentication, shared database, real message transmission or payment. No personal data is required.

All demo changes can be reset. The repository adapter provides a clear place to introduce a secured backend or a real CMS for a commissioned implementation.

The same care, on a smaller screen.

Real 390 × 844 browser captures. Images open at their original size.

Designed to evolve.

Small, explicit modules

Buildless HTML, CSS and ES modules at runtime. A shared build produces 75 readable static pages per language. Search, availability, content rendering, persistence and administration are separated into focused modules.

Images and fonts are hosted locally. The fictional map needs no third-party tiles, API key or paid service.

Measured quality

Complete Playwright journeys cover citizen services, content publication, safe previews, persistence and reset. Layouts are checked at 1920 × 1080, 1440 × 900, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844.

Automated accessibility checks support the implementation, but do not establish full RGAA compliance. Lighthouse measurements, test scope and limitations are provided in the verification report.

Read the verification report

All people, addresses, schedules, budgets, decisions and services shown in the municipal demo are fictional. Licensed photographs illustrate the imagined territory; their real sources and authors are listed in the photographic credits.
