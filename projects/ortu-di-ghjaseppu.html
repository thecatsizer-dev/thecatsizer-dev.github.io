Website and online store

L’Ortu di Ghjaseppu

A website and online store for a grower in Corsica. Customers can discover local produce and seeds, shop online and find a pickup point near them.

  • WordPress
  • WooCommerce
  • Responsive design
Visit website↗ See screenshots↓

Project details

Site design

The WordPress site puts products, pickup points and contact information within reach from the homepage, with a French and Corsican language selector.

WooCommerce store

The shop groups produce and seeds by category and provides a cart, checkout and order account.

Local pickup

A dedicated page lists pickup locations and shows the areas served on a map.
