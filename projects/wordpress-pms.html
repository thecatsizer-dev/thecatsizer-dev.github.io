Casa Suprana

An independent hotel concept on the Corsican coast. Eighteen rooms, a complete booking journey and a local hotel administration, presented in French, English, Italian, Spanish and German.

A house worth staying for.

A distinctive hotel experience

A coherent visual identity, locally hosted typography and licensed photography support the house, four room types, restaurant, spa, experiences, offers and regional stories.

Five complete language versions

Editorial content, booking, administration, forms and messages share one translation system. Direct language URLs and matching screenshots keep the portfolio and demonstration consistent.

One modular foundation

A shared build produces 120 static pages from one presentation layer. The booking rules and transactional inventory are shared across all five languages.

A booking journey that reaches confirmation.

Find the right stay

Dates, adults and children feed real local availability. Compare flexible and non-refundable rates with seasonal prices, minimum stays and promotional offers.

Make it your own

Add breakfast, a wellness ritual or a transfer. The itemised total includes every option and a simulated local tax. A declined trial payment creates no booking.

Return to a confirmed booking

A successful trial assigns a physical room and creates a reference. Find the stay again, change it or cancel it under the displayed conditions; cancellation releases its availability.

A room, a table and a memorable escape.

Restaurant menus and table bookings

Three seasonal menus with adult and child prices. Reserve a suitable physical table for a 90-minute sitting; overlapping bookings and restaurant closures are checked before confirmation.

Excursions with real local capacity

A coastal walk, a boat outing and a flavours workshop have their own times, prices and limited places. Trial payments, changes and cancellations update the same local inventory.

Managed from the same workshop

Manage menu prices, restaurant closures, activity prices and session capacities alongside hotel bookings. Tables and activities have their own references and are available under My booking.

Book a tableBook an activity

The same inventory, on both sides of the desk.

Manage the availability calendar, arrivals, departures, bookings, closures, rate periods, extras, promotions and the main content. Changes affect the public website in the same browser, including other tabs. Confirmed bookings retain their original quote.

Two providers. One clear boundary.

A self-contained public demo

The local provider works without an external API, subscription or server. IndexedDB transactions recheck the room and price before confirmation, preventing incompatible concurrent bookings in the same browser.

The existing Beds24 work, preserved

The original WordPress connector, availability page and video remain available. The new server-side API V2 transport handles tokens, scoped reads and guarded booking operations, with simulated-response tests.

An explicit production boundary

No Beds24 credentials are loaded by this website. A real connection requires the hotel’s authenticated server, property and rate mappings, business policies, payment setup and live-account validation. No real Beds24 reservation was created.

The autonomous booking engine is fully interactive. The Beds24 server adapter is tested with mocked responses; production activation and real account permissions remain to be validated.

Where the integration began.

Read the video transcript

The video introduces La Maison des Oliviers, a demonstration holiday rental in Corsica. A search for 22–24 November 2026 and two adults returns two available nights with a total price of €300. A second search for 25–27 November returns unavailable dates and invites the guest to choose another stay. The interface is shown in French, Italian and English, followed by the responsive mobile layout. Design and development by Giovinazzo José. This demonstration property does not create reservations or take payments.

From demonstration to a real hotel.

The identity, room content, seasons, rate plans, offers and extras can be adapted to a real property. Production delivery would add the owner’s secure PMS gateway, authenticated access, payment provider, operating rules and the required accessibility and privacy review.
