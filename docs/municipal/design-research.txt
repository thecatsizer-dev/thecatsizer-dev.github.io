DESIGN RESEARCH — VALMER-SUR-RIVE
References actually consulted on 24 November 2026 before implementation.

Sens — https://www.ville-sens.fr/
Observed: strong place identity, immersive territorial imagery, broad editorial
coverage and explicit access to municipal projects. Retained principle: give the
territory a recognisable visual language while explaining public investments.

Les Deux Alpes — https://www.mairie2alpes.fr/
Observed: prominent search and direct access to practical resident services.
Retained principle: everyday tasks must be visible before deep institutional menus.

Zonza / Santa Lucia — https://www.zonzasantalucia.corsica/
The search fetch failed, but the real website was opened and inspected in the
browser. Observed: Mediterranean identity, landscape-led opening, direct services
and municipal information. Retained principle: place and community can coexist.

Courtenay — https://www.courtenay.ca/
Observed: resident-oriented service categories, an explicit alerts area and useful
public information. Retained principle: prominent notices must remain distinct
from ordinary editorial content and must not obscure navigation.

Parramatta — https://www.cityofparramatta.nsw.gov.au/home
The search fetch was blocked, but the real homepage was opened in the browser.
Observed: clear needs-based navigation, dominant search and useful service entry
points. Retained principle: make information retrieval a primary interaction.

Official French awards — https://trophees-communication.com/palmares/
The 2025 list confirms Sens first in towns over 10,000 inhabitants, Les Deux Alpes
first under 10,000, and Zonza / Santa Lucia second under 10,000. International award
claims from the brief were not independently established and are not advertised.

ORIGINAL DIRECTION
Valmer uses a new river-and-banks mark, a fictional map, Newsreader and Inter,
deep blue, olive and restrained terracotta. The public site alternates an editorial
opening, a service band, asymmetric news, dated events, project explanations and
landscape storytelling. No source logo, page design, municipal text or award badge
is reused. The photography is licensed illustration with explicit real sources.
